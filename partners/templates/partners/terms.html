Draft v1 — must be reviewed by a lawyer before real commercial use

A Partner Company sells directly to buyers. RootLine does not collect buyer payments, hold funds, or arrange delivery.

Fees and accurate reporting

The platform owner configures your commission/subscription plan. The initial default commission is 5%; your assigned plan and any overrides determine the actual invoice. You must report final sold amounts accurately, including sales originating from the Partner Store that are completed by phone.

Audit and disputes

RootLine may review order requests, sale confirmations, supporting records and reported discrepancies. Completed sales are retained in an audit trail. Only the platform owner can adjust ledger records, with a recorded reason.

Billing and suspension

Pay invoices by their due date using the instructions on your billing page. Overdue invoices may hide your Partner Store after the configured grace period. This does not delete your records or settle the debt.

Reviews and responsibility

Only a buyer with a completed order may leave a verified-purchase review. Fake reviews, self-reviews and misleading claims are prohibited. Companies remain responsible for their products, certificates, payments and deliveries.

This draft is a feature demonstration, not legal advice or a finalized contract.
