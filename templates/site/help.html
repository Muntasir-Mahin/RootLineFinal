A LITTLE HELP

Help & frequently asked questions

Buying, selling and learning on RootLine.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

How do I find a product?

Use Search RootLine in the navigation to search product names, descriptions and categories, plus published Agro Updates. The marketplace also has price and category filters.

Browse the marketplace
How do I become a seller?

Create an account, open My account and select Become a Seller. Staff review your request before you can list products.

How can I get help with an order?

Sign in and open My orders. Select the order to review payment and delivery details. If there is an issue, use the dispute option when available and explain the problem for staff to review.

Open my orders
How are payments handled?

RootLine records manual bKash and Nagad payments. Submit the transaction details shown on your order. Staff verify funds; a website status alone does not transfer or refund money.

Why can't I record a voice question?

Sign in, open Ask RootLine and check the voice status below the question box. Recording needs microphone permission and HTTPS or localhost. When microphone access is unavailable, you can upload a WebM, M4A/MP4, WAV or Ogg recording instead.

The server also needs ffmpeg, whisper.cpp and the multilingual Whisper small model. If the page says voice is unavailable, the site owner must finish this setup. After it is ready, select Check connection. Keep recordings under 60 seconds and 8 MB; review the transcribed text before sending.

Do voice questions and AI answers use a paid service?

Voice transcription uses local Whisper. Answers and photos use local Ollama with Gemma. They are separate services, so transcription can work even if the answer model is offline. AI answers may be mistaken; confirm crop treatments with a local agricultural officer.

How do dark mode and cookie preferences work?

Use the theme button in the header. Signed-in users save the preference to their account; visitors save it in their browser. Essential cookies support sign-in and secure forms. Campaign tracking is optional and can be changed using Cookie preferences in the footer.

Contact & order support

For a purchase problem, open your order and use its dispute option when available so staff can review the details.
